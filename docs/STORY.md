# Main Story — "The First Signal" (working title)

Builds on [`docs/WORLD_LORE.md`](WORLD_LORE.md) (setting/terminology) and ties into the MVP scope in [`docs/TECHNICAL_PLAN.md`](TECHNICAL_PLAN.md) (§1 below is what we actually need written and quest-scripted for the MVP milestone; §2-3 are the long-term arc so future content has somewhere to go).

## 1. MVP arc — Act 1: "First Contact" (single Shard)

This is the story content that must exist for the MVP milestone (one Shard, one hub, one dungeon, ~20-50 concurrent players).

### Inciting incident
The player character starts as an ordinary person who experiences a "glitch" — a brief, involuntary glimpse of the Continuum (a flicker of impossible geometry, a sound that isn't there). This has always happened to a small fraction of people; most dismiss it. The player instead reaches back, and the glitch resolves into a door.

They cross into their first Shard and immediately meet their first Emergent — not captured yet, just... present, curious, watching. This is the tutorial: learning to approach, calm, and eventually bond with it. The starter Emergent is chosen here (species options TBD in the creature roster work).

### Key beat structure (quest chain, single Shard)

1. **Arrival** — tutorial: movement, basic interaction, first glimpse of a wild Emergent.
2. **The Bond** — tutorial: capture mechanic taught via the starter Emergent (framed as a mutual choice, not a conquest — matches Pillar 1's "partnership" framing).
3. **Signal from Origin** — the player is contacted by **Dessa Vail**, a Concordat liaison, who explains (briefly, not lore-dumped) what Anchors are and invites the player to Origin.
4. **First Surge** — a small, low-stakes Null Surge threatens a native Emergent population in the starting Shard. Player's first real combat test and first sense of stakes: the Null aren't abstract, they hurt things the player has started to care about.
5. **Rho's warning** — the player meets **Rho**, a long-retired Anchor who was among the first to ever cross into the Continuum. Rho is unsettled: recent Surges are more frequent and more organized than anything in decades of Continuum activity. Someone or something may be deliberately widening the connection between the worlds. This is the hook into the long-term arc (§2) — planted here, not resolved.
6. **Rival introduction** — **Kess**, a confident, competitive Anchor close to the player's own experience level, challenges the player to a friendly battle after the Surge is dealt with. Establishes the PvP arena framing narratively and gives a recurring rival for future content/tournaments.
7. **First dungeon** — the MVP's single instanced dungeon, framed as investigating a denser-than-normal Null concentration tied to Rho's warning. Ends on a cliffhanger sighting: a uniquely stable, unusually intelligent Null entity observing from a distance, then vanishing. (This is the seed for §2's central antagonist figure — do not resolve or explain it yet.)

### Tone notes for this act
Curious and welcoming, not grim. The Null are a real threat but the emotional center of Act 1 is the growing bond with the starter Emergent and the found-family energy of Dessa/Rho/Kess — the mystery is a background hum, not the main event yet.

## 2. Long-term arc (post-MVP, multi-Shard) — outline only

Not needed for MVP, but documented now so future content additions have continuity instead of being invented ad hoc.

- **The central mystery**: something is deliberately widening the boundary between our world and the Continuum, causing Null Surges to escalate in frequency and coordination beyond their natural baseline.
- **Thren** — the uniquely stable, intelligent Null entity glimpsed at the end of the Act 1 dungeon. As the story develops across future Shards, it becomes clear Thren isn't simply malicious — current working theory (subject to change as we write more): Thren is what happens when an Anchor bond is attempted and fails catastrophically, leaving a fragment of a person's intent fused into corrupted data. This keeps the antagonist sympathetic/tragic rather than a generic evil final boss, consistent with the "avoid ultimate-evil framing" note in `WORLD_LORE.md` §7.
- **Rho's backstory**: seeded for later — Rho's unease is personal, not just professional. Left open for a future reveal (e.g. Rho was involved in whatever incident created Thren) rather than committed to now.
- **Structure**: each future Shard/content update can carry one "chapter" of this arc (a piece of the mystery, a Thren sighting or confrontation, escalating Null Surge severity) without requiring a full rewrite of the main quest — matches the technical plan's phased Shard rollout (§6 of `TECHNICAL_PLAN.md`).

## 3. Recurring characters reference

| Name | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Dessa Vail** | Concordat liaison, Origin-based quest-giver | Warm, organized, the "onboarding" voice of the game's authority structure |
| **Rho** | Retired first-generation Anchor, mentor figure | Knows more than they initially say; long-term arc's emotional anchor (pun intended) |
| **Kess** | Rival Anchor, PvP framing | Competitive but not villainous; recurring tournament/arena rival across updates |
| **Thren** | Central Null antagonist figure | Not a generic evil boss — tragic origin to be developed; appears rarely, escalates over future content |

## 4. Guild & side-content narrative hooks (later)

- Guild halls in Origin can carry their own light narrative framing ("Bonds"/"Circuits" per `WORLD_LORE.md` §6) — e.g. guild-specific bounty boards tied to larger, multi-party Null Surge events.
- Lore collectibles (data-fragments found in Shards) can drip-feed background on the Continuum's origin and on Thren without requiring dialogue-heavy main quests — good low-cost content for filling out world depth over time.
