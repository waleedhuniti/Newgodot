# Species brief: Ferrowing (Tier 3 — Radiant line)

Redesign brief replacing the uploaded reference model, which was identified as a likeness of Omnimon/Omegamon (a copyrighted Digimon character) and will not be used in the game. This brief keeps the general power fantasy (imposing armored dragon-knight, final evolution tier) while changing every element that made the original recognizable as that specific character.

## What to change from the reference, and why

| Reference element (Omnimon-identifying) | Change to |
|---|---|
| White/blue plate armor color scheme | **Deep obsidian-black armor with molten-gold trim** — matches the Radiant type's palette direction (light/gold, not the original's cool white/blue) |
| Dragon-head-shaped claw glove fused onto one arm | **Remove entirely.** Replace with a plain clawed gauntlet wreathed in a constant small flame/light particle effect — no secondary head/face shape anywhere on the arms |
| Wolf-head cannon fused onto the other arm | **Remove entirely.** Replace with a smooth, faceted crystal spike growing from the forearm (asymmetry kept, but as a natural crystalline growth, not a mechanical weapon-head) |
| Single center forehead horn | **Twin swept-back horns**, asymmetric length (one longer than the other) — breaks the single-horn silhouette |
| Segmented bat/dragon membrane wings | **Layered feather-like plate wings** (metallic, overlapping segments like feathers rather than a bat membrane) — different silhouette and material read |
| Large held broadsword | **Twin short energy-glaives** (one in each hand instead of one big sword) — changes the weapon silhouette and removes the one-sword-forward held pose |
| Blocky, heavily plated mecha-knight proportions | **Leaner, more draconic proportions** — narrower torso, longer neck, less blocky joint armor, more like an armored dragon that stands upright than a robot knight |

## Identity

- **Name**: Ferrowing
- **Type**: Radiant (see `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` §4.1)
- **Tier**: 3 (final evolution of its line)
- **Silhouette read**: tall, narrow, imposing — reads as "a dragon that learned to stand like a knight," not "a knight wearing dragon parts"
- **Color**: obsidian-black base, molten-gold trim, twin glaives glowing pale gold
- **Personality/lore flavor**: per `docs/WORLD_LORE.md`, an Emergent this stable and powerful would be extremely rare — a good candidate for a signature "aspirational" Tier 3 that most players work toward rather than an early-game form
